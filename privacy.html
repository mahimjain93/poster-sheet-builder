Privacy Policy

Poster Sheet Builder · Last updated 22 November 2026

Poster Sheet Builder (“the app”) is a free, personal tool at poster-sheet-builder.vercel.app that arranges images onto print-ready poster sheets. This page explains what data the app handles and how.

Images you add

Images you add are processed entirely in your web browser. They are not uploaded to or stored on any server run by the app. Project files you save are downloaded to your own device.

Pinterest connection (optional)

If you choose to connect a Pinterest account, the app asks Pinterest for read-only access (scopes user_accounts:read, boards:read and pins:read) so it can list your boards and import the images from pins you select. Specifically, the app:

  • reads your board names and the pins on boards you choose, including their image links and sizes;
  • uses that data only to import images into the app for you to arrange and print;
  • does not create, edit or delete anything on your Pinterest account;
  • does not sell, share or use your Pinterest data for advertising or any other purpose;
  • keeps the Pinterest access token only as long as needed for your session and does not store your Pinterest data on its servers.

You can disconnect the app at any time from your Pinterest account settings (Security and logins → Apps).

Analytics and cookies

The app does not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking. The hosting provider (Vercel) may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times, for security and reliability.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
